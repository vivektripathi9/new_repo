Overview

Godrej Florenne - 4 & 5 BHK Luxury Row Houses At Whitefield, Bengaluru

Godrej Florenne is an ultra-luxury, low-density gated community by Godrej Properties, situated off Soukya Road in the prime growth corridor of Off-Whitefield, East Bengaluru. Spanning across ~20 acres of lush landscaped greens, this exclusive private address features a limited collection of 242 ultra-premium row houses designed for high-end luxury living. Designed with opulent specifications, the residences offer expansive, light-filled interiors, private outdoor gardens, rooftop terraces, centralized air conditioning, and internal private elevators for seamless vertical living. The project is strategically positioned to offer excellent connectivity to major business parks like ITPL and EPIP Zone, the Kadugodi Metro Station (Purple Line), Whitefield Railway Station, Kempegowda International Airport via key express routes, as well as nearby international schools, shopping destinations, OMR, and Sarjapur Road.
